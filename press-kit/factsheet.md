## One line

A sci-fi metroidvania set seven years after HELIOBANE: map six worlds on frozen star roads with an arm that moves heat.

## Short description

FARSTRAND is a sci-fi metroidvania set seven years after HELIOBANE. The sun is still going out, and the only roads off its worlds are the ones the hive grew between the stars, frozen now into glass. Hesper Lind, cartographer of the seventh survey, wakes on Halm with an arm of black glass that takes heat and gives it back. Freeze the hive's creatures into platforms, grow its buds into bridges, overfeed it until it bursts, and draw the map of six worlds nobody came back from. It stands on its own; HELIOBANE players will hear an old radio in the glass.

## Pillars

- **The Graft moves heat**: Umberflesh comes in three states: sunfed, flesh and glass. Quench takes a step of heat out, Kindle puts one back, and the arm holds only so much. Freeze an enemy and stand on it, or feed it until it bursts.
- **The map is her work**: Hesper is a surveyor. Rooms are pencilled in as you enter them and inked when you rest at a beacon. The old marks scratched into the glass are the hint system, and you can turn them off.
- **Built for a pad and the Deck**: Every screen was made at 1280×800 with a pad in hand: on a Steam Deck the 320×180 picture fills the screen at exactly four times size. Everything can be remapped.

## Features

- A metroidvania across six worlds and a hub, with a Herald in each world
- The Graft: Quench and Kindle work on enemies, walls and buds; Overfeed bursts a sunfed enemy
- Nine guns in three damage types, a weapon wheel, and Aurum to spend at Old Mott's stall
- Bob the drone: Soundings, vents, switches, and later a swap
- A map that draws real rooms in pencil until you rest at a beacon, with an old-marks hint system you can turn off
- Story, Normal and Hard, plus four assists you can turn on one at a time: damage taken, boss telegraphs, hazards, healing drops
- 320×180 pixels, 32 colours, tracker music, 60 updates a second
- Pad first, made for the Steam Deck: its 1280×800 screen shows the game at exactly four times size. Everything is remappable
- English, Japanese and Turkish text. The prologue is narrated in all three; Old Mott, Josef Kell and the Worn speak English and Japanese

## History

FARSTRAND is Gand's second game in the HELIOBANE universe, after the shoot 'em up HELIOBANE. It started on 25 September 2026 from one brief: a metroidvania in the same universe, across several planets, in HELIOBANE's art style, playable on the Steam Deck from the first build. A short proof of concept on Halm came first; the full game followed in four milestones.
